About

Cookie Pulse is a live terminal for Cookie Chain — a Solana-compatible SVM with sub-second finality and ~$0.000005 fees per transaction. Every number on this page is read directly from rpc.cookiescan.io and the public Cookiescan APIs at request time; no mock data. Pools are enumerated on-chain via getProgramAccounts across Cookieswap DAMM, Cookieora DAMMv2, CookieBox DBC and the Meteora bonding-curve fork. Connect any Solana wallet (Nightly, Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) to inspect balances and write a real transaction to Cookie Chain.
